About JRC

Since 1994

Built From Scratch
in Los Angeles

JRC Culinary Group was founded with one vision: to bring authentic, restaurant-quality Asian dumplings to the frozen food market.

Operating out of our facility in Monterey Park, we manufacture premium dumplings and frozen Asian foods using authentic recipes rooted in tradition.

We're building toward something big — a nationally recognized brand that stands alongside the best in Asian frozen food.

Heritage

Our Story

根源与传承

JRC Culinary Group began with recipes passed down through our family for generations. Growing up around Cantonese dim sum, dumplings were always part of our lives.

Founded in the San Gabriel Valley to bring those same authentic recipes to professional kitchens. Located in Monterey Park, one of the most vibrant centers of Chinese food culture in America.

Every dumpling reflects the same care, precision, and handmade quality that our family practiced for generations.

"Our goal is to become the defining Asian frozen food brand in America — built right here in Los Angeles."— JRC Culinary Group
